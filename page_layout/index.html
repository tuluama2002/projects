Brisbane Mission

The Australia Brisbane Mission is one of the best missions in Australia. It is in Queensland and is recently divided into two missions north and south. Missionaries there serve by inviting others to come unto Christ by helping them receive the restored gospel through faith in Jesus Christ and his atonement, Repentance, Baptism, Receiving the Gift of the holy ghost and enduring the end.

Missionaries in the Brisbane are young adults ages 18-30 years of age from different cultures and backgrounds. They work hard to build relationships with God’s Children that had not received the gospel, serve their communities, help individuals and families find peace, hope, and happiness through the gospel. The mission is not only for the people that had not receive the gospel but also for the missionaries as well to develop their knowledge in the gospel and strengthen their faith and relationship with the savior. Through service, consistency, discipline, prayer, and daily Scripture study they will become more like the savior.

Missionaries around the world are the most nicest people ever, their intentions are to help others to come unto the savior so they can receive eternal life, and taste the fruit of eternal happiness.
