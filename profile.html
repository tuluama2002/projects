Whats interesting about Kiribati?

Kiribati is a small island in the pacific, barely seen on the map with 100,000 people. This island is an only island in the world that covers 4 parts of the hemisphere.
It is also the first country to see first sunrise world widely. Most people label kiribati as a sinking country but it's not actually true.

Important part of kiribati culure:

  1. Hospitality → how they treat their guest is very important
  2. Forgiveness → they adapt to christianity and practice that
  3. Giving → how they show their love to others or love language

Kiribati is an amazing place, it's people are known as the most humble people in the pacific. Hospitality, loving and forgiving is the most important part of their culture.
